## 简介

一隅书架首个正式版本。面向个人与家庭的自托管数字书库，将散落在 NAS、家庭服务器或本地硬盘中的电子书、PDF、漫画和有声书统一整理、检索与沉浸阅读。

### 核心能力

- **多格式导入**：支持 EPUB、PDF、漫画（含 CBZ 与图片文件夹）以及有声书的导入与监控目录自动发现。
- **有声书合并**：可将单个 M4B、M4A、MP3 或多分轨音频合并为有声书，提供章节与轨道切换、倍速、音量、睡眠定时、跨页面连续播放与独立进度同步。
- **图书与音频分离**：书库可按电子书、漫画、有声书分类呈现，互不干扰。
- **阅读体验**：内置网页阅读器，支持书目、章节与卷册识别，阅读进度全端同步。
- **数据私有**：书库数据库、账户、阅读进度与系统设置全部保存在本地，原始读物继续保留在用户指定目录，不依赖第三方云端。

## Overview

The first stable release of booknook, a self-hosted private digital library for individuals and families. It organizes ebooks, PDFs, comics, and audiobooks stored on a NAS, home server, or local drive into a unified, searchable, and immersive reading experience.

### Key capabilities

- **Multi-format import**: EPUB, PDF, comics (including CBZ and image folders), and audiobooks with watched-folder discovery.
- **Audiobook merging**: merge single M4B, M4A, MP3 files or multiple audio tracks into audiobooks, with chapter/track switching, playback speed, volume, sleep timer, cross-page continuous playback, and per-book progress sync.
- **Media-type separation**: browse your library by ebook, comic, and audiobook categories without mixing.
- **Reading experience**: a built-in web reader with series, volume, and chapter recognition and synchronized reading progress across devices.
- **Private by design**: the library database, accounts, reading progress, and system settings stay on your own device; original books remain where you put them, with no dependency on third-party cloud hosting.
